Why MR.WASH IT

Not a car wash. A paint care ritual.

Every vehicle goes through a fixed 9-stage process — the same one we use on show cars. No spinning brushes, no swirl marks, no shortcuts.

  • Touchless pre-soak & snow foam

    Grit is lifted off the clear coat before a single mitt touches your paint.

  • Reclaimed, filtered water

    Up to 80% of every litre is recycled on site — spot-free rinse, guilt-free wash.

  • Hand-finished, always

    Plush microfibre drying and a hand-applied sealant on every package above Basic.
